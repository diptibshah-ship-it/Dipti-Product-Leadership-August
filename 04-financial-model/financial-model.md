## 1. Business case

_Why this initiative is worth funding over the alternatives. Include the key unit economics assumptions, CAC, LTV, payback period, where relevant._

| Assumption | Value | Source / rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Target customer segment** | Mid-market B2B SaaS (ARR $1M–$50M) | Playing to Win: highest immediate expansion potential |
| **Cohort size (Year 1)** | 40–50 new mid-market customers | Conservative estimate: 3–4 per month starting ramp |
| **CAC** | $15K–$20K per customer | Blended: self-serve + direct sales hybrid; 2–3 month sales cycle |
| **ASP (initial annual contract value)** | $35K–$50K per customer | Turnkey product tier + core integrations |
| **LTV** | $140K–$180K (4-year window) | Calculated at 115% NRR by Year 2; expansion from integrations and upsells |
| **LTV:CAC ratio** | 8–10x | Healthy for B2B SaaS; expansion revenue significantly improves payback |
| **Payback period** | 4–6 months | CAC recovers within 1st contract; LTV expansion upside unlocked in months 7–12 |
| **Gross margin (Year 1)** | 70–75% | Product delivery + managed integrations; R&D and infrastructure amortized across customer base |
| **Time-to-First-Value reduction ROI** | 3-month payback on TTFV acceleration engineering | Reducing TTFV from 21 → 7 days directly increases activation and expansion velocity |
| **Investment required** | $2.5M–$3M for Year 1 | 8–10 FTE (product, eng, CS, integrations) + 3–4 FTE sales/marketing (CS-led motion); infrastructure and tooling |
| **Expected Year 1 ARR** | $1.7M–$2.3M | 45 customers × $38K avg ASP (conservative expansion uplift already included) |
| **Expected Year 2 ARR** | $4.5M–$5.5M | Cohort 2 (new customers) + NRR expansion from Cohort 1 (at 115% by Q4 2026) |
| **Payback on Year 1 investment** | 12–16 months (cumulative) | Revenue ramp curves upward in Q4 2026 as activation and TTFV benefits compound |

**The case in one paragraph:**

Meridian Foundations is a high-ROI bet on our highest-confidence market—mid-market SaaS vendors who value speed, predictability, and platform lock-in over bespoke customization. By reducing Time-to-First-Value from 21 to 7 days and automating onboarding with production-ready integrations, we directly solve the #1 adoption blocker mid-market customers face. This speed directly drives a projected Activation Rate increase (35% → 55%) and Net Revenue Retention expansion (100% → 115%), creating a flywheel: faster customers → higher NRR → higher LTV → 8–10x LTV:CAC ratio. A $2.5M–$3M Year 1 investment yields $1.7M–$2.3M ARR and pays back the investment within 12–16 months, with significant upside in Year 2 as cohort expansion compounds. This motion is capital-efficient, de-risks our product-market fit narrative, and positions Meridian as the default platform for mid-market foundations.

## 2. Kill criteria

_The specific signals that would tell you this bet is no longer worth pursuing. Be explicit about the metric, the threshold, and the timeline._

| Metric | Threshold | Trigger | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Activation Rate (Day-14 sustained usage)** | Does not reach 40% by Q2 2027 (6 months in) | Signals onboarding or product-market fit gaps; current 35% baseline should improve to 45%+ with TTFV work | Kill the mid-market motion; pivot to enterprise; revisit onboarding hypothesis |
| **Median Time-to-First-Value** | Still >14 days by Q2 2027 (after engineering sprint 1 + automation) | TTFV reduction is the core value prop; failure to deliver it undermines the entire adoption strategy | Refocus engineering on root-cause friction; consider partnership vs. build trade-off |
| **CAC Payback Period** | Exceeds 9 months by Q2 2027 (above our 4–6 month target) | Signals customer acquisition is too expensive or ACV is too low for this segment | Reevaluate pricing; shift GTM to higher-touch (enterprise); or reduce marketing spend and grow slower |
| **Net Revenue Retention** | Remains at 100% or declines by Q4 2026 (no expansion uplift) | NRR is our expansion growth engine; flat NRR means we're losing the compounding advantage | Revisit CS motion and integration roadmap; shift from expansion back to pure adoption |
| **Year 1 ARR run-rate** | Falls below $1.2M by Q4 2026 | Revenue should compound to >$1.7M by year-end; <$1.2M signals weak customer demand or long sales cycles | Kill Year 1 cohort and redeploy team to a faster-growing segment or use case |
| **Customer satisfaction / NPS** | NPS <40 or churn >20% annual for mid-market cohort by Q3 2026 | Unsustainable product satisfaction undermines NRR and expansion; signals product-market fit failure | Pause new sales; invest in customer success and product fixes for 6–8 weeks |
| **Team execution velocity** | Cannot ship 80%+ of committed onboarding automation by Q2 2027 | Technical risk: if engineering cannot deliver core features, the ROI thesis collapses | Escalate to leadership; hire additional engineering capacity or reduce scope ruthlessly |

**Summary decision rule:**

If **two or more kill criteria are breached by Q2 2027**, we will **pause the mid-market motion entirely** and reallocate the team to higher-potential opportunities (e.g., enterprise with higher ACV, or a different product bet). If **only one criterion is breached and remediable** (e.g., CAC payback), we will **invest an additional 2–4 weeks in targeted fixes** before re-evaluating. If **all metrics are on track or exceeded**, we will **double down in Year 2** with an expanded team and GTM budget.
