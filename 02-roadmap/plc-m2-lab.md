# Prioritization & Roadmapping, Module 2 Lab

## Prioritize your Rocks and your Hard Nos

- **🪨 Rock #1:** Ship production-ready SDKs and integrations for top 10 ecosystems (Stripe, Twilio, AWS, Segment, etc.) to materially reduce Time-to-First-Value from 21 → 7 days
- **🪨 Rock #2:** Build automated provisioning and onboarding flows to increase Activation Rate (day-14 sustained usage) from 35% → 55%
- **🪨 Rock #3:** Launch CS-enabled expansion playbook (upsell/cross-sell motion) and expand NRR from 100% → 115% among existing mid-market customers
- **❌ Hard No #1:** No bespoke professional services or deep enterprise-only customizations as a primary revenue path; keeps team focused on product-led mid-market motion
- **❌ Hard No #2:** No single-tenant or heavily regulated compliance builds that would require special handling outside our core mid-market SaaS segment
- **❌ Hard No #3:** No reactive feature factory; every roadmap item must directly trace to one of our three KRs (Activation, TTFV, NRR)

## Show and swap

- **Do the Rock selections feel traceable to a clear strategy, or do they read like a feature list?:** ✅ Clear strategy traceability. Each Rock directly maps to a KR from our Playing to Win cascade:
  - Rock #1 (SDKs/integrations) → KR2 (reduce TTFV to 7 days)
  - Rock #2 (automated onboarding) → KR1 (increase Activation Rate to 55%)
  - Rock #3 (CS-enabled expansion) → KR3 (increase NRR to 115%)
  These are strategic bets about *how* we drive adoption and expansion, not a grocery list of features.

- **Pick one Hard No and make the case for why it should actually be a Rock.:** Hard No #2 (no single-tenant/regulated compliance builds) makes the most tempting near-term case: one large enterprise prospect has explicitly asked for HIPAA compliance. However, defending the Hard No: shifting to single-tenant infrastructure would block 80% of our mid-market target (who run on shared SaaS platforms). The 1–2 large deals won't move our NRR metric the way expanding our existing 50 mid-market customers will. Stick with the Hard No.
