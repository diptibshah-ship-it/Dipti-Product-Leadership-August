# Team Charter: Meridian Foundations (B2B · adoption + expansion)

> Module 3 · Lead and Develop High-Performing Teams, ★ Deliverable 3
>
> Define how your team operates. Two completed components: **What We Own** and **How We Decide**.

## 1. What We Own

_The team's mandate: the outcomes and surfaces this team is accountable for end-to-end, and the explicit edges where your ownership stops._

| Area | We own it | We influence it (don't own) |
|---|---|---|
| **Time-to-First-Value (TTFV) for new customers** | End-to-end onboarding funnel, SDK/API developer experience, out-of-the-box integrations, provisioning automation, docs/quickstart quality | Enterprise sales cycle, deal structure, deployment environment constraints |
| **Customer Activation (day-7 sustained usage)** | Feature discoverability, in-app guidance, sample data, tutorial quality, SDKs and API examples | Customer's internal adoption decisions, engineering resourcing on their side |
| **Expansion/upsell motion (NRR growth)** | Product usage analytics, expansion-ready features, upsell-triggering workflows, CS playbooks, success metrics visibility | Sales compensation, customer success staffing, renewal pricing decisions |
| **Developer platform reliability & quality** | SDK stability, integration testing, documentation accuracy, API design, performance SLAs | Customer's infrastructure, enterprise security/compliance audits, industry regulations |
| **Customer insights & feedback loops** | Usage data, feature request triage, onboarding friction analysis, expansion pattern identification | Quarterly business reviews (QBRs) owned by CS, executive escalations owned by Sales leadership |

> Our mission in one line: **Enable mid-market SaaS vendors to adopt Meridian Foundations and expand revenue predictably by reducing friction from evaluation → production and making expansion seamless.**

## 2. How We Decide

_The team's decision-making operating model: the decisions you make, who makes each call, and how disagreements resolve._

| Decision type | Who decides | Who's consulted | How we break a tie |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Feature prioritization for KR1 & KR2 (Activation & TTFV)** | Product Lead (Meridian Foundations) + Eng Lead | Sales (customer requests), CS (friction signals), Design (UX impact) | Product Lead has final call; escalate to Group VP if Eng Lead objects on resourcing grounds |
| **SDK/API design & breaking changes** | Eng Lead (platform architecture) | Product, Design (usability), Technical Writers (documentation), customers (direct survey) | Eng Lead decides; Product can escalate to CTO if customer impact is material and Eng is blocking needed speed |
| **Onboarding experience & trial flow** | Product Lead | Design, CS (trial-to-paid insights), Eng (feasibility) | Product Lead; defer to Design on UX/copy final call; if Eng says "can't ship in Q", Product re-scopes with Eng Lead |
| **Customer expansion workflows & pricing tier features** | Product Lead + Sales (revenue impact) | CS, Eng, Finance | Consensus or co-decision (Product + Sales); escalate to VP Sales if conflict |
| **Bug severity & SLA responses** | Eng Lead | Product, CS (customer impact) | Eng Lead; P1 bugs always get triage; P2/P3 are triage-and-queue. Customer escalations go to CS lead for decision on urgency |
| **Roadmap sequencing across adoption & expansion initiatives** | Product Lead | Eng Lead (capacity), Sales (GTM timing), CS (customer health signals) | Product Lead drafts; Eng Lead confirms feasibility; VP Product approves at month-start planning |

> Our default: **Product Lead decides on product roadmap and feature prioritization; Eng Lead decides on technical architecture and resourcing; Sales partner decides on expansion packaging.** We escalate to VP Product when a decision blocks two or more teams from their OKRs.

## 3. Escalation path (optional, but recommended)

_When we disagree on how to proceed, or when a decision spans ownership boundaries._

- **Level 1 (DRI + stakeholders):** Owners of the two conflicting areas meet and try to decide. Time box: 1 business day.
- **Level 2 (VP Product):** If unresolved, VP Product mediates and decides, with input from both sides. Time box: 2 business days.
- **Level 3 (Group VP / VP Engineering):** Only for decisions that materially change resource commitments or product direction. This goes to monthly steering or standing leadership review.

## 4. Rituals that reinforce "How We Decide"

- **Weekly sync (45 min):** Product Lead, Eng Lead, CS Lead. Review TTFV and Activation metrics, surface blockers and customer friction, decide on week-ahead pivots.
- **Bi-weekly prioritization (60 min):** Product, Eng, Design, 1 Sales rep. Groom next 2 weeks of backlog; Eng commits to sprint; Product confirms sequencing.
- **Monthly roadmap review (60 min):** Expanded team + VP Product. Review 3-month roadmap, re-forecast, decide on any major shifts. Sales & CS bring win/loss & churn signals.
- **Monthly escalation checkpoint:** Any "stuck" decisions from the month are surfaced and resolved here.
